# Power BI Reporting Automation for Spreadsheet-Heavy Business Teams

## Project context

Selected project experience from my previous employment as a Data Analyst, across a combined energy-services and automotive-distribution reporting engagement.

The combined engagement covered three business units and 16 spreadsheets. Teams needed recurring reporting across operational, procurement, inventory, sales and HR data.

## Reporting problem

Data was fragmented across spreadsheet sources. Repeated consolidation and calculations made recurring executive reporting time-consuming.

## My contribution

- Consolidated operational and business data from fragmented spreadsheets.
- Applied SQL validation and Power Query transformations to prepare the reporting data.
- Used Power BI modelling and reusable DAX measures to standardise reporting calculations.
- Supported executive reporting, scheduled delivery and dataset validation.

## Outcome across the combined engagement

The engagement delivered **11 executive reports** and removed **approximately 10 hours of weekly consolidation effort**.

These are results across the combined engagement. My contribution included the data preparation, validation, modelling and reporting work described above.

Power BI dashboard implementation, Excel reporting automation, spreadsheet consolidation and improvements to recurring management reporting.
